# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in
Herdr World a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback; and
- taking responsibility, apologizing to those affected, and learning from
  mistakes.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, sexualized
language or attention, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or
political attacks, publishing others' private information, or other conduct
that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and
may take any action they consider appropriate and fair in response to
unacceptable behavior. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments,
commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of
Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban a contributor for behavior
they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers will apply this Code fairly and consistently. They will protect
the privacy of the reporter of an incident to the extent possible and will not
publish private details without consent unless required by law.

## Scope and reporting

This Code applies within project spaces and when an individual is officially
representing the project. If you observe abuse or have a concern, report it
privately to the project owner at the contact in `SECURITY.md` or `GOVERNANCE.md`.
Do not use public Issues for sensitive reports.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will document the decision and the reasons for a material
enforcement action in the project records, with private details redacted. A
contributor may ask the owner for a review of an enforcement decision.
