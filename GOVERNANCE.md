# Governance and maintainer authority

Herdr World is an independent project owned by Yaswanth Narvaneni for
pre-existing and Yaswanth-authored original World work. Contributors retain
copyright and attribution for their own contributions under the applicable
Apache-2.0 or inherited MIT scope. The project does not claim ownership of
Herdr, Herdr Web, Claw-Empire, PixiJS, fonts, or other dependencies.

## Authority

The project owner currently has final authority to:

- approve, freeze, extend, or close World specifications;
- decide World branding and non-endorsement wording;
- accept or reject contributions and appoint or remove maintainers;
- approve releases, compatibility matrices, and release artifacts;
- coordinate private security response and supported-version decisions; and
- resolve governance or Code of Conduct appeals.

Maintainers may review and merge work within an approved specification,
operate CI and releases delegated to them, and prepare upstream proposals.
They may not change an approved contract, transfer upstream ownership, or
represent downstream policy as Herdr or Herdr Web policy.

## Decision records

Material decisions belong in an issue, pull request, numbered specification,
provenance record, or release record. The record should identify the scope,
owner, evidence, compatibility impact, and rollback or follow-up. Public
upstream proposals are tracked independently; an open or declined upstream
proposal does not block a downstream release.

## Contributions and conduct

There is no CLA. Contributions require DCO sign-off as described in `DCO.md`.
The project follows `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`. Security reports use the private
process in `SECURITY.md`; support questions and reproducible bugs use the
separate channels in `SUPPORT.md`.
